Entry for the [Describe Your Ex in One Word](https://make.fun/c/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7) bounty on make.fun — sum up an ex in exactly one word.

## The word

> **Unsubscribe**

One click and they're out of your inbox forever — no reply-all, no "we need to talk", just gone from the mailing list of your life.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the word deterministically to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), directly to the final file — no temp files, no trailing newline. It validates the gate before writing: non-empty, exactly one word, at most 111 characters.
